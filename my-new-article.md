# How to Get Your Ex Back: A Respectful Guide to Reconnecting.

Silence. That's the word I would use to describe the feeling of losing someone. Your phone, when you type their name and delete it. That table at your favorite restaurant.

That one thought you have before you fall asleep, that someone isn't there. Everyone who has been in love knows that feeling. Very few of them will take the time to really sit with that feeling and figure out what it means.

## Start With Yourself, Not With Them

I won't go into any manipulation techniques. There is nothing here about how to get them jealous or how to make them think about you by doing certain things on social media.

This will simply be my advice on whether or not you should try to get your ex back, and if you do want to, here are some steps to follow. First off, why do you want them? Sit with that question for a while. Not why do you want your ex, but why do you want that specific person back in your life?

You probably have some sort of answer, and it's most likely because you miss them. Just because you miss someone doesn't mean they're good for you. You may just be grieving someone you loved.

## Give the Silence Room to Breathe

People tend to remember the good times when someone isn't around. They start to block out all the bad memories and only focus on the times they made you smile. Do you miss that person, or do you miss who they were when things were going great? Those are two very different answers, and only one will lead you to wanting to give that relationship another chance.

Ask yourself why you two broke up. Was there a certain incident, or did you just not communicate properly for a while? These are things that can be fixed. Maybe you weren't on the same page when it comes to life goals, or maybe you both just aren't compatible when it comes to love.

Those are things that can't be fixed, and you shouldn't want that person in your life. Once you have determined that you truly want that person back, give it some time. Don't text them right away. Give it at least a few weeks, if not more.

## The Art of the First Message

If you try to reach out right after a breakup, chances are you'll have a bad conversation, and neither of you will walk away feeling better. By not reaching out right away, you're giving them the opportunity to think about how they feel about you as well. When you decide to reach out, you want to do it from a position of strength, not weakness. The you that wants to reach out to your ex when they've been out of your life for a month is a lot cooler and more collected than the you that reaches out right after breaking up.

Take that time and spend it with your friends. Get back into things you may have stopped doing when you were in that relationship.

![A quiet desk set up for self-reflection](https://user-images.rightblogger.com/ai/088adff5-93f0-4958-979b-80fd9acf65cb/quiet-desk-self-reflection-cac920ba.jpg)

You'll find that you become a more attractive person when you have other things going on in your life. When you reach out, keep it simple. You don't want to send them a long message telling them everything that you've learned about yourself since you two broke up.

Keep it light and just try to initiate conversation. Reach out about something you both enjoyed, or maybe tell them about a book that you think they would like. Or ask them about something they told you they were working on.

## If They Respond

Whatever you do, make sure it's not generic. Tell them about something you both have in common. Show them that you know them as a person and not just your ex. Expect them not to reply, or maybe they do reply and are nice to you but don't give you any further information. Don't take it personally if they do either. If they do reply and seem to want to talk more, don't overdo it. You want to keep things moving slow and allow them to open up to you.

Try asking questions about their day and see what they have to say. Just treat them like you would any other girl you may be talking to. If things progress to you two meeting up in person, keep it short. Meet up for coffee or something you both enjoy doing.

Don't make it feel like a date. Just hang out and have fun. Eventually, you'll need to bring up what happened between you two. Maybe it's not the best idea, but it's necessary.

## When It Does Not Go the Way You Hoped

Find a way to talk about why you broke up and what you can do to make it work this time. Make sure you are listening to what they have to say as well. They may tell you no or just leave you on read. Whatever they do, don't push them to change their mind.

If they don't want to be in your life, then let them go. Trying to force someone into your life is never going to end well for either of you. Learn to accept that some people aren't meant to be with you. It's better to let them go than to have them in your life and be unhappy.

Getting your ex back is not the hardest part of this whole situation. Once they are back in your life, you need to learn from your mistakes. If you break up again, you need to fix what caused the problem the first time. Hopefully, this helped you decide whether or not you should try to [get your ex back](https://clearerconnections.com/how-to-get-your-ex-back/).

## What This Is Really About

Remember to take your time and learn from your mistakes.

So the real guide, underneath all of this, is not about how to get your ex back. It is about how to become someone who [approaches love](https://clearerconnections.com/), including the love you have already lost, with honesty, patience, and care. That version of you is the one most likely to build something that lasts, whether with this person or eventually with someone else. And it is, regardless of how the story ends, the version most worth becoming.
