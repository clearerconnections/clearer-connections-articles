## Why Did You Break Up in the First Place?

[Going back to your ex](https://clearerconnections.com/how-to-get-your-ex-back/) is something many people will experience in their lifetime. So before you decide to text that "I've been thinking about you" here are some things to consider. What caused you to break up?

This is the number one question you should be asking yourself. People tend to forget the reasons they broke up in the first place. We tend to remember the good times and not the bad. All those fun memories you had with your partner cloud your judgment.

Remember why you broke up and analyze the situation. Did you break up due to external factors. Maybe one of you went to college or you were both going through difficult times. These situations can change.
